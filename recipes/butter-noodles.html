Butter Noodles

    Ingredients
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • 1 oz Noodles
  • 6 tbsp Butter
  • 1/3 cup Parmesean Cheese
  • Herbs, Salt, Pepper, to taste
    Instructions
  1. Heat 2 quarts of water with 1 tablespoon kosher salt in a large pot on high heat until boiling. Add the egg noodles. Cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Reserve 1/4 cup of the pasta cooking liquid and drain the noodles.
  3. Place the drained hot noodles in a large bowl. Add the butter and Parmesan and stir until the butter has melted. Stir in a tablespoon or two of the pasta cooking water.
  4. Stir in the chopped parsley. Add salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste. Serve immediately.
