Air-Fryer Sausage

original recipe

    Ingredients
  • 6 italian Sausages
    Instructions
  1. Poke* the sausages in 3 places each with a sharp knife, breaking through the casing.
  2. Arrange the sausages in a single non-overlapping layer in the air fryer.
  3. Cook at 400°F until well browned and no longer pink inside, 9-12 minutes.
