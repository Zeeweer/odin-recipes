Christmas Crackers

original recipe

    Ingredients
  • ~42 Saltine or Club Crackers
  • 1 Cup of Butter
  • 1 Cup of Brown Sugar
  • 1 tsp of Vanilla (Optional)
  • 2 Cups of Chocolate Chips (Semi-sweet or Milk Chocolate)
  • 1 1/2 Cups of Toppings like: chopped nuts, candies, sprinkles, coconut, sea salt, dried fruit, or pretzels
    Instructions
  1. Preheat your oven to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Line a baking sheet (15" x 10") with aluminum foil and spray with cooking spray to avoid sticking.
  3. Place crackers in an even layer. (This was 7 rows by 6 rows with Club Crackers).
  4. In a medium saucepan, melt butter and brown sugar over medium heat, stirring often.
  5. Bring to a boil and boil for approximately three minutes, stirring constantly.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla if desired. Pour toffee mixture over top of crackers. Spread evenly over top with a spatula. Bake uncovered for 5 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat and top evenly with chocolate chips. Wait 5 minutes to melt chocolate chips. (Place in warm oven for one to 2 minutes if needed to melt chocolate chips further if needed)
  8. Gently spread chocolate out over toffee using a spatula.
  9. Immediately top with any toppings such as pretzel pieces, small candies, dried fruit, sprinkles, coconut or nuts.
  10. Cool 10 minutes before transferring to the refrigerator. Refrigerate at least 2 hours or until very firm. Break into larger pieces. Store cracker toffee in an airtight container for up to two weeks.
